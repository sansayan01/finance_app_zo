# MicroFlow Pro — Product Launch Video Brief

## Intent

A 60–90 second product promo for individual money-lenders in India. The hook is the pain point every lender knows by heart: notebook chaos, missed collections, wrong interest calculations. The payoff is MicroFlow — a simple app that replaces all that with clean digital records. Sell the relief, not the features.

## Audience

Individual money-lenders (sahukars), age 28–40, semi-urban, WhatsApp + Facebook only, low tech comfort. They track loans in notebooks or scattered Excel. Hindi/Hinglish comfortable. Decision driver: "dusra use kar raha hai" trust signal.

## Format

- Aspect: 9:16 vertical (WhatsApp/Facebook shareable)
- Length: 60–90 seconds
- Style: Premium Indian fintech — warm colors, clean typography, smooth motion
- Tone: Relatable problem → simple solution. No jargon. No SaaS speak.

## Customizations

- **angle**: Pain-point story (notebook chaos → app solution)
- **VO_MODE**: Restructured per scene (not verbatim script)
- **flow**: automation (no storyboard, build direct)
- **destination**: WhatsApp / Facebook vertical
- **Language**: Hindi/Hinglish with English loan words (byaj, borrower, collection) — natural for the audience
- **Music**: Offline BGM (MusicGen) — warm, Indian-fintech feel, not corporate
- **Voice**: Kokoro offline TTS — warm male Hindi/Hinglish voice (`bm_0` or similar)
- **no-capture**: true (no website URL to capture; app UI will be described in asset notes)
