# Bengali Facebook Posting Rules and Metrics Guide

---

## Section 1: Posting Rules

### Best Posting Times and Rationale

| Time Slot | Day | Rationale |
|-----------|-----|-----------|
| ৭:৩০ AM | Monday–Friday | Rural and semi-urban audience is waking up, checking phones before heading to work or the market. Highest open-rate window for working-class Bengali users. |
| ১২:০০ PM | Saturday & Sunday | Weekend leisure scrolling. Users have free time, they are more likely to engage with longer captions and leave comments. |
| ৮:০০ PM | Monday–Friday | After dinner, family time. Users scroll Facebook casually. Engagement is good but slightly lower than morning — better for educational content. |

**Primary recommendation:** Post reels between ৭:৩০ AM and ৮:০০ AM on weekdays. Saturday posts around ১২:০০ PM work well for community engagement. Never post after ৯:০০ PM — the audience is off Facebook by then.

### Posting Frequency Per Week

- **Minimum 4 posts per week.** Two reels + two image/carousel posts.
- **Ideal 5 posts per week.** Three reels + two static posts.
- **One day off per week.** Sunday or Monday — whatever gives you a full rest day.
- **Holiday posts are optional.** If a national holiday falls on a weekday, that day acts as your rest day. Do not force post on public holidays — the algorithm penalizes low engagement on holidays.

### Content Ratio (Pain : Education : Solution : Trust)

| Content Type | Ratio | Weekly Target | Example |
|-------------|-------|---------------|---------|
| Pain/Problem | ২৫% | ১ post/week | "মাসের শেষে টাকা নেই কিভাবে বাঁচাবেন?" |
| Education/Tip | ২৫% | ১ post/week | "৫ টিপস যেগুলো মাসে ৫০০ টাকা বাঁচায়" |
| Solution/Product | ৩৫% | ২ posts/week (include at least ১ reel) | "এই অ্যাপটি দেখুন — মাত্র ২ মিনিটে হিসাব" |
| Trust/Security | ১০% | ১ post every ২ weeks | "আমাদের কোনো ফি নেই, আপনার ডেটা নিরাপদ" |

**Note:** This is a guideline, not a rigid formula. If a week has heavy product messaging, balance with an extra pain or education post to avoid being overly promotional.

### What NOT to Post (Legal-Safe, No Over-Promising)

1. **No income guarantees.** Never post "এই টুল দিয়ে মাসে ১০,০০০ টাকা আয় হবে" or any similar income promise. Use "বাঁচানো যায়", "হিসাব সহজ হবে", "খরচ কমাতে সাহায্য করে" — never "আয় বাড়বে" or "লাভ পাবেন".
2. **No loan approval promises.** Never say "লোন নিয়েছেন" or "১০০% Approbation". Say "লোনের হিসাব করা যায়", "খরচ দেখা যায়".
3. **No before/after money shots.** No screenshots of bank balances, no "আগে ০ টাকা বাঁচত, এখন ৫০০০ টাকা". This violates Facebook's financial services ad policy and looks dishonest.
4. **No competitor bashing.** Never mention or compare with any other app or service.
5. **No fake urgency.** "শুধু আজকে" or "সীমিত সময়ে" are not allowed for free tools. Use only when there is a genuine limited-time offer.
6. **No direct money links.** Never link to UPI, bank transfer, or any payment page on Facebook. Direct users to the app or website only.
7. **No medical or legal claims.** This is a financial awareness tool, not a financial advisor. Add a small disclaimer if needed: "এটি শুধু একটি হিসাবিং টুল, বৈধ আর্থিক পরামর্শ নয়।"

### When to Skip a Day

- Public holidays in Bangladesh (Eid, Pohela Boishakh, Victory Day, etc.)
- When you have published 2 consecutive reels — give at least one static post day between reels to let engagement settle.
- When Facebook analytics show your last post had abnormally low reach (< ৩০ সংখ্যক ব্যক্তি reach) — skip the next day and post a fresh hook the day after.
- When you are traveling or your team is unavailable. Better to skip one post than to schedule a low-energy post that gets poor engagement and confuses the algorithm.

## Section 2: Engagement Rules

### Response Time Target

- **Reply to every comment within ৩০ মিনিট (30 minutes).** The Facebook algorithm rewards fast comment replies with higher post reach. A reply within ৩০ মিনিট can boost a post's distribution by ২০–৪০% compared to replies after ২+ hours.
- **If ৩০ minutes is not possible, set a reminder every ১৫ মিনিট** to check new comments. Batch reply at ৩০ মিনিট intervals.
- **Do not let a comment sit unanswered for more than ২ ঘণ্টা (2 hours).** After 2 hours, the comment thread dies and the algorithm stops pushing that post.
- **Night-time comments can be replied early next morning**, but always within the same 24-hour window they were posted.

### Pin Strategy

- **Pin ONE comment on every reel/story post.** Always pin the most engaging or most helpful comment. This sets the tone for the entire comment section.
- **What to pin:**
  - A positive customer comment (e.g., "ছোটখাটো ব্যয় দেখানো হচ্ছে হ্যাঁ, খুবই সহজ!")
  - Our own CTA comment (e.g., "ডেমো চাইলে কমেন্টে 'খাতা' লিখুন")
  - An educational tip that adds value beyond the post itself
- **Never pin a complaint or negative comment.** If someone is unhappy in their comment, do not pin it. Either reply to it kindly and do NOT pin it, or if it violates guidelines, hide it.

### Pin Visibility (Hide Negative / Pin Positive)

1. **Hide rule:** If a comment contains spam, irrelevant links, offensive language, or competitor promotion — hide it. Do not engage publicly; just hide and move on.
2. **Negative-but-legitimate comments:** Reply professionally and do NOT pin. Let the reply sit naturally in the thread. If the comment is about a genuine concern (e.g., "কি এটা নিরাপদ?"), reply with the trust template and let the reply sit un-pinned.
3. **Pin only one comment per post.** Pinning multiple comments creates clutter and confuses the algorithm on which comment signals "value."
4. **If you accidentally pin a low-quality comment, unpin it immediately** and pin the correct one. No penalty for correcting a pin mistake.

### How to Engage Without Sounding Salesy

1. **Lead with a question, not a pitch.** Instead of "আমাদের অ্যাপটি ডাউনলোড করুন", try "আপনার মাসে কতটুকু বাঁচিয়ে পান? কমেন্টে সংখ্যা দিন।" — this invites a conversation, not a purchase.
2. **Use "আপনি" (you) more than "আমরা" (we).** When users feel addressed personally, they engage more and respond less to hard sell.
3. **Give before asking.** Every CTA comment should follow a value comment. First give a tip, then ask if they want more. "প্রতিদিন ৫ মিনিট হিসাব রাখলে মাসে সহজে বাঁচানো যায়। আপনার কি এই পদ্ধতিটা চেষ্টা করতে চান? কমেন্টে বলুন।"
4. **Celebrate small wins publicly.** When a user says they saved টাকা, reply with encouragement, not with a sales pitch. "অসাধারণ! আপনি কতদিন ধরে চালিয়ে এলেন সেটা জানি না, কিন্তু শুরুটাই সবচেয়ে কঠিন অংশ।"
5. **Never say "BUY" or "BUY NOW."** Use "চেষ্টা করুন", "দেখুন", "আজকে শুরু করুন" instead of "কিনুন" or "পে করুন". We are giving a free tool — frame it as sharing, not selling.
6. **Reply with the same casual, friendly tone** as the user's comment. If the comment is in informal Bangla (Hinglish mixed), reply in the same register. Do not use formal business language as it creates distance.
7. **Reply to follow-up questions within the same thread.** If someone asks "কিভাবে ব্যবহার করবো?" after your tip, stay in that thread and give a full answer in ২-৩ lines. Do not redirect them to DMs for a simple answer.

### How to Fill Each Column

**Week #:** Simple sequential numbering starting from ১.

**Reels Posted:** Count of reel/video posts published that week. Target is ২-৩.

**Static Posts:** Count of image, carousel, or text-only posts published that week. Target is ২-৩.

**Total Reach (FB Analytics):** Go to Facebook Creator Studio or Meta Business Suite → Content → select the week → total reach number. This is the unique number of people who saw any of your posts that week. Write the number only (e.g., ১৫,৩৪০). If reach drops more than ৫০% from previous week, add a note about what changed.

**Comments Containing "খাতা":** Use Facebook's comment search or manually count. This is your conversion signal — every "খাতা" comment means the user is ready for a demo. Target: minimum ৫-১০ "খাতা" comments per week.

**DMs Received:** Count of direct messages received on Facebook that week related to the product/tool. Include DMs from both page and profile.

**WhatsApp Conversations Started:** Count of people who clicked the WhatsApp link in your DM and started a conversation. This is the truest measure of engagement — they want to talk in real time. Target: minimum ৩-৫ new WhatsApp conversations per week.

**Top Performing Post (Hook):** Write the hook text (first ৩-৫ words) of your highest-reach post that week. Example: "ভাগবেন, অনেক মানুষ..." — write it exactly as it appeared as the opening line. This helps identify which hooks resonate.

**Notes/Observations:** Anything unusual — a spike from a shared post, a comment section that went viral, a post that underperformed for unknown reasons, a competitor's activity you noticed. This is your journal for patterns.

## Section 4: Iteration Plan

### Weeks ১-২: Baseline — Just Post and Measure

**Goal:** Establish a rhythm. Identify what the audience responds to.

- Post **minimum 4 posts per week** (mix of reels and static).
- Use the caption templates from the captions file — do not write original captions yet, just pick from the bank and post.
- Do not touch Hashtag Bank yet — use only Primary hashtags on every post.
- Engage on every comment within 30 minutes.
- **Do not optimize anything yet.** Do not cut posts, do not change times. Just observe.
- At the end of Week ২, fill in the tracking table and review the numbers.

**What to look for in Week 1-2 data:**
- Which day/time got the most reach?
- Which caption template got the most "খাতা" comments?
- Which content type (reel vs static) got more DMs?
- What was the average reach per post?

### Weeks ৩-৪: Optimize — Cut What's Not Working, Double What Works

**Goal:** Refine based on data from Weeks 1-2.

- **Double down on what worked.** If pain posts got ৩x more "খাতা" comments than education posts — post ২ pain posts per week instead of ১.
- **Cut what did not work.** If static posts got minimal reach and no comments — move those slots to reels. If a specific caption template consistently got zero "খাতা" comments, archive it.
- **Experiment with timing.** If morning posts (৭:৩০ AM) performed poorly but evening posts (৮:০০ PM) got ২x reach — shift your posting time. Test one new time slot this week.
- **Add Secondary hashtags.** Start rotating Secondary hashtags (use ৩-৫ per post). Monitor if specific hashtag sets correlate with reach jumps.
- **Review pin strategy.** Check pinned comments — did pinned CTA comments drive more "খাতা" responses than pinned positive comments?

**What to look for in Week 3-4 data:**
- "খাতা" comment count went up or down compared to baseline?
- Reach per post increased, decreased, or flat?
- WhatsApp conversations per week increased?
- Any specific reel format (hook style, visual style) that consistently outperformed?

### Month ২+: Scale What's Working

**Goal:** Turn winners into systems and expand reach.

1. **Create a "winner repeat" system.** Identify your top ৫ caption templates and top ৫ hooks from Weeks 1-4. Always keep these in rotation. Never post without at least one proven winner in the pipeline.
2. **Increase reel frequency if reels outperformed.** If reels consistently delivered more reach and more "খাতা" comments than static posts — shift to 3 reels/week and ২ static posts.
3. **Test Secondary hashtags in depth.** Run a 2-week experiment where every post uses ১ specific Secondary hashtag rotated systematically. Track which hashtags drive the most profile visits or DM requests.
4. **Introduce seasonal/topical hashtags.** When a relevant occasion approaches (Eid, Pohela Boishakh, etc.), add the seasonal hashtag to ৩-৪ posts that week and monitor engagement lift.
5. **Start A/B testing CTAs.** Post the same content with two different CTA templates on different days. Compare which CTA drives more "খাতা" comments. Keep the winner.
6. **Document what works in a simple "winners doc."** Every Friday, write down the ২-৩ best posts of the week with their hook text, content type, reach, and "খাতা" count. This becomes your content playbook for the next month.
7. **Consider paid boost for proven winners.** If a post organically got ৫x more reach than your average and drove ৫+ "খাতা" comments — consider boosting it with a small.facebook ad budget (৳৫০০-১০০০/Bangladeshi Taka). Start small. Measure DM conversion from boosted posts separately.

**Warning — Do NOT Scale Yet If:**
- Your "খাতা" comment count is still below ৫ per week — go back to Weeks 1-2 mode and fix the content hook first.
- Reach is steadily declining week over week — pause new posts, audit what changed, and fix before scaling.
- WhatsApp conversations are not increasing — the DM script may need adjustment, or the "খাতা" pipeline is broken upstream.
