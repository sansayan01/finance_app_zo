# Bengali Facebook Organic Strategy Design — MicroFlow Pro

**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Owner:** Sayan Mondal  
**Channel:** Official MicroFlow Pro Facebook page  
**Region:** West Bengal first; pan-India later  
**Status:** Approved strategy direction; ready for asset creation after review

---

## 1. Goal

Increase MicroFlow Pro's Facebook organic reach among Bengali/West Bengal audiences and turn engagement into qualified demo leads.

The campaign should not feel like generic SaaS marketing. It should feel like a local, practical solution for people who already manage lending, savings, and collection records manually.

Primary outcome:
- More organic reach through Bengali reels and comment engagement.

Secondary outcome:
- Convert interested commenters into WhatsApp demo conversations.

---

## 2. Positioning

MicroFlow Pro will be positioned as:

> **MicroFlow Pro — সুদের ব্যবসা, সঞ্চয় আর আদায়ের খাস ডিজিটাল খাতা।**

Avoid positioning it as:
- fintech SaaS
- NBFC/MFI software
- bank/lending platform
- formal finance compliance system

Use the familiar mental model:
- khata
- notebook
- local lending records
- savings records
- collection records
- SMS and PDF proof

This keeps the message understandable for low-tech and semi-urban audiences.

---

## 3. Target Audience

Use mixed positioning, but keep each individual post focused.

Primary audience segments:
1. Individual local money-lenders / সুদের ব্যবসায়ী
2. Small savings or collection group managers
3. Small local collection offices
4. Small business people who maintain loan/savings records in notebooks

Shared audience traits:
- Bengali-speaking, West Bengal first
- Facebook + WhatsApp heavy
- Lower comfort with technical SaaS language
- Trust-driven decision making
- Familiar with paper khata and manual calculation pain
- More likely to respond to relatable problems than polished feature claims

---

## 4. CTA Strategy

Use a comment-first CTA:

> **ডেমো চাইলে কমেন্টে লিখুন “খাতা”।**

Flow:
1. Reel asks viewers to comment `খাতা`.
2. Page replies quickly to increase engagement.
3. Page sends DM.
4. DM moves them to WhatsApp demo.

Why this CTA:
- Comments help Facebook organic reach.
- It is less cold than asking for immediate WhatsApp click.
- The Bengali keyword feels local and memorable.
- It creates an easy lead-qualification signal.

Do not use direct app signup as the main CTA yet. The audience needs trust and a demo before conversion.

## 5. Content Pillars

| Pillar | Share | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Pain/problem | 35% | Stop scroll by naming the viewer's daily problem |
| Education/tips | 25% | Build trust and authority without selling every time |
| Product solution | 25% | Show MicroFlow as the simple fix |
| Trust/proof/CTA | 15% | Convert interest into comments and demos |

### Pillar Examples

Pain/problem:
- “আজ কার কিস্তির তারিখ ছিল?”
- “খাতায় লেখা আছে, কিন্তু খুঁজতে কত সময় লাগে?”
- “সুদ আলাদা, কিস্তি আলাদা, সঞ্চয় আলাদা — মাথা খারাপ হয়ে যায় না?”

Education/tips:
- “সুদের ব্যবসায় ৩টা হিসাব আলাদা রাখতেই হবে।”
- “প্রতিটা আদায়ের পরে প্রমাণ রাখুন।”
- “মাসের শেষে নয়, প্রতিদিন হিসাব মিলান।”

Product solution:
- “গ্রাহক টাকা দিলে SMS যাবে, হিসাবও সেভ থাকবে।”
- “Loan, savings, collection — এক জায়গায়।”
- “PDF report এক ক্লিকে।”

Trust/proof/CTA:
- “আপনার হিসাব শুধু আপনার কাছেই থাকবে।”
- “ডেমো চাইলে কমেন্টে লিখুন ‘খাতা’।”

## 6. Weekly Rhythm

Recommended posting schedule:

| Day | Content Type | Intent |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Pain reel | Reach and relatability |
| Tuesday | Bengali tip post / carousel | Trust and saves |
| Wednesday | Product solution reel | Explain one feature through one problem |
| Thursday | Trust/education post | Reduce fear and build credibility |
| Friday | CTA/comment reel | Drive comments and DMs |
| Saturday | Optional poll/story/repost | Light engagement |
| Sunday | Rest or story only | Avoid overposting |

Do not overload the page with only product demos. The page should feel useful first, sales-focused second.

---

## 7. Reel Formula

Every reel must stay under 25 seconds.

Structure:

| Time | Job |
|---|---|
| 0-3 sec | Painful hook / question |
| 3-15 sec | One concrete problem |
| 15-21 sec | MicroFlow solution |
| 21-25 sec | Comment CTA |

Rules:
- One reel = one pain point only.
- Avoid feature dumping.
- Avoid generic business motivation.
- Keep language local and simple.
- Make the first line strong enough to read even with sound off.
- Use captions in Bengali on screen.

---

## 8. Bengali Tone Rules

Use:
- দাদা
- খাতা
- হিসাব
- বাকি টাকা
- কিস্তি
- সঞ্চয়
- আদায়
- SMS
- PDF রিপোর্ট
- ডিজিটাল খাতা

Avoid:
- SaaS
- CRM
- automation
- fintech platform
- dashboard-heavy language
- “grow your business” style generic copy

Voice should feel:
- local
- practical
- slightly urgent
- respectful
- not over-polished
- not legally risky

Use “দাদা” sometimes, not in every reel, so it does not become repetitive.

---

## 9. Trust and Legal-Safety Layer

MicroFlow should be described as a record-keeping/digital khata tool, not a lending provider.

Safe framing:
- “আপনার হিসাব রাখার অ্যাপ”
- “loan/savings/collection record রাখুন”
- “SMS/PDF প্রমাণ রাখুন”

Avoid risky claims:
- “loan recover guaranteed”
- “interest legally correct”
- “we verify customers”
- “bank/NBFC alternative”
- “collection pressure” language

Trust line to repeat every 3-4 reels:

> **আপনার হিসাব শুধু আপনার কাছেই থাকবে।**

## 11. Success Metrics

Track weekly:
- Reel reach
- 3-second views
- average watch time
- comments containing `খাতা`
- DMs started
- WhatsApp demo conversations
- demo bookings
- follows from reels

The strongest early success metric is not followers. It is:

> **How many people comment `খাতা` and move into WhatsApp demo.**
