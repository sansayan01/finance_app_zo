## Overview

MicroFlow Pro is a digital lending ledger for Indian money-lenders. Track loans, calculate interest, manage borrowers, send SMS reminders — all from your phone.

Our pricing is built for Indian sahukars who want affordable, simple tools without enterprise overhead.
