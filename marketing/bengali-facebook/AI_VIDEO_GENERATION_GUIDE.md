# AI Video Generation Master Blueprint — MicroFlow Pro Reels

> **Fixes for AI Video Generation Issues (Google Veo 3.1 / Kling / HeyGen / ElevenLabs):**
> 1. **Character Voice Swapping Fix:** Separate prompts per character shot (Customer clip vs Lender clip).
> 2. **Dialogue Truncation Fix:** Strict 8–10 Spoken Bengali Words Max per 10-second reel.
> 3. **VFX & Text Overlay Fix:** AI generates raw camera footage; text badges & subtitles added in CapCut/InShot.

---

## 🎬 Master 2-Shot Workflow for Every Reel

### SHOT A: Customer Conflict Clip (0.0s – 3.5s)
* **Visual Prompt:** `Cinematic 9:16 vertical video, 24fps. Close-up shot of an angry 42yo Bengali borrower in a simple shirt, slamming hand on a wooden desk with a red ledger, looking furiously at camera in a local Indian shop.`
* **Spoken Audio (Customer Only — Max 4 words):** `"আমার টাকা কোথায় দিলেন?!"`
* **On-Screen Subtitle (CapCut):** `আমার... টাকা... কোথায়... দিলেন?! 😳`

---

### SHOT B: Lender Solution & CTA Clip (3.5s – 10.0s)
* **Visual Prompt:** `Cinematic 9:16 vertical video, 24fps. Medium shot of a calm 32yo Bengali money-lender in a neat linen shirt holding a smartphone, showing green timestamped entry on screen, smiling confidently.`
* **Spoken Audio (Lender Only — Max 8 words):** `"এই দেখুন, এন্ট্রি সেভ আছে। সুদের ব্যবসায় কমেন্টে লিখুন 'খাতা'।"`
* **On-Screen Subtitle (CapCut):** `এই দেখুন... এন্ট্রি সেভ আছে! ✅` + `কমেন্টে লিখুন 'খাতা' 👇`

---

## 🛠️ Post-Production Checklist (CapCut / InShot — 30 Seconds)

1. **Stitch Shot A + Shot B** back-to-back in CapCut.
2. **Auto-Captions:** Click *Text → Auto-Captions → Bengali* (generates 1-word pop-in captions).
3. **Add Text Badges:** 
   - Shot A: Red box with text `"১২,৫০০ টাকা বাকি?!"`
   - Shot B: Green box with text `"তারিখ ও সময় সমেত সেভ ✅"`
4. **Export:** 1080×1920 (9:16 Vertical), 30fps.
