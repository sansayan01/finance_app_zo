## Why Gamification Works

Field staff are the backbone of any MFI. Keeping them motivated is critical. Gamification taps into intrinsic motivation — the desire for progress, recognition, and friendly competition.

## Core Mechanics

### Streaks

A streak tracks consecutive days of meeting a target. Missing a day resets the streak, creating a powerful incentive to show up every day.

### Achievements

Achievements unlock when staff hit milestones — first 100 collections, 30-day streak, zero missed payments in a month. They provide a sense of progress beyond daily targets.

### Leaderboards

Leaderboards create friendly competition between agents and branches. When done right, they boost performance without creating unhealthy pressure.

## Implementation Tips

- Keep targets achievable — 80% of staff should hit them on a good day
- Celebrate achievements publicly (within the team)
- Rotate leaderboard categories to give different strengths a chance to shine
- Never tie gamification directly to compensation — it kills intrinsic motivation

## Results from the Field

Organizations using gamification report:

- **25% increase** in daily collection rates
- **15% reduction** in staff turnover
- **Higher customer satisfaction** from more consistent service
