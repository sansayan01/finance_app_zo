## 🛠️ Raw UGC Shooting Rules for Optimum Performance

1. **Lighting & Audio:** No professional softboxes or external microphones required. Room light + natural voice ambient sound performs 3x better than studio audio for UGC reels.
2. **Text Placement:** Keep text overlays in the **middle 60% of the screen** so Facebook/Instagram reel UI buttons (Like, Share, Caption) do not block the words.
3. **Pacing:** Never leave a static frame for more than 1.5 seconds. Rapid transitions between visual cuts keep watch time above 85%.
